Integra pairs AI-driven support with clear progress tracking to help Poway Recovery Center clients stay engaged and connected.

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard cols-2" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header" style="text-align: center;">Why Poway Recovery Center</div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Community Impact</strong>
        <p>PRC serves local individuals in recovery but lacks digital tools to track progress or stay connected to support.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>Service-Learning Fit</strong>
        <p>Partnering with PRC lets us apply AI and full-stack skills to a real, high-impact community need.</p>
    </div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--standard" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell ocs__grid-cell--header" style="text-align: center;">Planned Features</div>
</div>

<div class="ocs__grid ocs__grid--color" style="margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🎵 Mood Room</strong>
        <p>Mood check-ins paired with therapeutic music.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🤖 AI Recovery Bot</strong>
        <p>Trauma-informed support and resource connections.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📝 Wellness Assessments</strong>
        <p>Weekly check-ins on sleep, anxiety, hope, and focus.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📊 Progress Dashboard</strong>
        <p>Visual trends across recovery milestones.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🚨 Counselor Alerts</strong>
        <p>Automated flags when trends decline.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>🖼️ Recovery Gallery</strong>
        <p>Before/after stories from real journeys.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>💬 Reflection Wall</strong>
        <p>Anonymous space to celebrate small wins.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
        <strong>📅 Meeting Scheduler</strong>
        <p>Availability tracking and easy sign-up.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="ocs__grid-cell">
